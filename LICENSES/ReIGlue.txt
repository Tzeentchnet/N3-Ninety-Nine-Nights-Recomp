ReIGlue
A fork of ReXGlue for Forza recompilations, starting with Forza Horizon 1:
ReImagined.
https://github.com/FH1-ReImagined/ReIGlue

Earlier N3 v0.10 development builds incorporated three title-independent
changes from ReIGlue: texture-watch validity (0008), the shared-memory
valid-range fast path (0031), and corrected D3D12 fence HRESULT checks (0408).
Those patches were retired with the ReXGlue v0.11 GPU rewrite. This notice is
retained for the historical source and binary releases that contained them.
